How it works

Three layers of detection. Nothing gets missed.

The extension reads what your Fiverr tab has already loaded — three independent signals, deduplicated so one message means one alert.

📡

Live data read

Reads the message and notification data your Fiverr page fetches on its own. Full buyer name, full message text, no truncation.

🔖

Unread watcher

Fiverr rewrites the browser tab title the instant something is unread. Cheapest signal there is — and it never misses one.

📋

Briefs, not just messages

Buyer briefs never appear in your inbox — only in the notifications panel. That panel is watched too, so briefs reach you tagged and unmissable instead of sitting there unseen.

🗂️

Every account named for you

Run one Chrome profile per Fiverr account. Every alert shows that account's Fiverr username, so you always know which inbox lit up. Nothing to set up.

🙈

Your replies stay quiet

Outgoing messages are filtered out, so you're only pinged when a buyer actually writes to you — not when you hit send.

🔒

Set up in a minute

Paste your Telegram bot token, send your bot one message, and you're done. There is nothing else to configure.

Is it safe?

By default it only reads. It never touches your account.

Most notification tools log into Fiverr with a bot and get accounts flagged. This one is a passive reader sitting inside a session you created by hand. Your traffic to Fiverr is identical to a person with the inbox open — because that is exactly what it is. The one exception is the optional Reply from Telegram feature. It is off until you switch it on, and it types your own reply into your logged-in Fiverr tab and presses Send. That is automation, which Fiverr's rules may restrict, so try it on one account first and use it at your own risk.

✗ What it never does (by default)

  • Never sends a request to Fiverr
  • Never logs in or automates your account
  • Never clicks, replies, or submits anything
  • Never polls Fiverr's servers on a timer
  • Never stores or transmits your Fiverr password
  • Never types or sends anything on Fiverr unless you switch on Reply from Telegram

✓ What it actually does

  • Reads data your own browser already received
  • Watches your tab title for unread changes
  • Sends the alert to your Telegram bot
  • Keeps settings in your browser's local storage
  • Runs only while your browser is open
Setup

Running in about five minutes.

No account to create, no card, nothing to install beyond the extension itself.

Download and unzip

Grab the zip below and extract it somewhere permanent — Documents\LynchAlerts is fine. Chrome loads it from that folder, so don't delete it afterwards.

Load it into Chrome

Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked, and select the unzipped folder.

Create your Telegram bot

Message @BotFather on Telegram, send /newbot, follow the prompts, and copy the token it gives you. Paste it into the extension and press Save.

Say hi to your bot

Open your new bot in Telegram and send it any message. Telegram forbids bots from messaging you first — skip this and every alert fails with "chat not found".

Detect and test

Press Detect chat ID, then Save, then Send test. A message should land on your phone straight away.

Pin your Fiverr inbox

Open fiverr.com/inbox and pin the tab. The extension's popup should show Watching: your_username. That line is your health check — if it names your account, you're live.

Running several Fiverr accounts? Create one Chrome profile per account, install the extension in each, and paste the same bot token everywhere. Every alert is labelled with the account it came from, and they all arrive in the same Telegram chat.

Updating

Never uninstall. Just replace the files.

Your settings — bot token and chat ID — live in your browser, not in the extension folder. Replace the files and everything carries over untouched.

Download the new version

Use the download button on this page. You'll get a fresh .zip.

Find your existing extension folder

The folder you picked when you first ran Load unpacked. Forgotten where it is? Open chrome://extensions — the path is printed on the extension's card.

Unzip over that same folder

Extract the new files into the folder you already have, replacing what's there. Same folder, same location — don't create a new one, and don't move it afterwards.

Reload the extension

Open chrome://extensions and click the ↻ reload icon on the card. Restarting Chrome does the same job. The version shown in settings should now be the new one.

✗ Don't uninstall and reinstall

It's more work and it deletes your settings — you'd have to redo the whole Telegram setup from scratch. Replacing the files keeps everything. For the same reason, don't move or rename the extension folder after installing: Chrome treats a moved folder as a brand new extension and your settings go with the old one.
Why isn't this automatic? Chrome only auto-updates extensions installed from the Chrome Web Store — anything loaded unpacked has to be replaced by hand. That's Chrome's rule, not a limitation of this extension. The built-in update checker is there so you always know when there's something new to grab.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Will this get my Fiverr account banned?

The extension never sends a single request to Fiverr — no logging in, no automation, no polling. It only reads what your browser already downloaded while you were sitting there. From Fiverr's side there is nothing to see beyond a normal person with their inbox open. That said, no third-party tool can carry a guarantee, which is why by default it touches Fiverr as little as physically possible: not at all.

The exception is the optional Reply from Telegram feature (off until you switch it on). It types your reply into your Fiverr tab and presses Send, which is automation, and Fiverr's rules may restrict that. It is switched on per account, starts in test mode and asks you to confirm every send, but nobody can promise how Fiverr treats it. Try it on one account first.

Does it work when my computer is off?

No. It runs inside your browser, so a browser has to be open with a Fiverr tab in it. If you need alerts around the clock, leave the browser running on a machine that stays on. This is a deliberate trade — the alternative is a server logging into your Fiverr account, and that is exactly the thing that gets accounts flagged.

Why Telegram and not WhatsApp?

WhatsApp has no free way to send yourself automated messages. The official API charges per conversation and requires pre-approved templates; the free workarounds either need WhatsApp Web logged into every browser profile, which risks your number, or rely on relay bots that WhatsApp bans every few months. Telegram is free, instant, has no message limits, and cannot be taken away. The alert reaches your phone identically — just from a different app.

Is there a backend? What data do you collect?

Alerts go straight from your browser to your own Telegram bot, so buyer messages never pass through our server. Your settings live in your browser. If you create the optional extension account, we store your email, a scrambled (hashed) password, your synced settings (including your Telegram bot token and chat ID, so they follow you) and when you were last active. If you switch on Reply from Telegram, your replies pass through our server and wait there (up to 3 days) until your extension collects them, then they are deleted. The extension also sends an anonymous once-a-day ping (a random install ID, the version and your alert channel, nothing else) so installs can be counted. No analytics on the site, no tracking of what you do, and we never see your Fiverr password.

Why does Chrome say "Load unpacked" and developer mode?

Because it's distributed directly rather than through the Chrome Web Store. It's the standard way to install an extension you have the source for — and you do: every file is plain, readable JavaScript you can inspect before loading it.

How do updates work? Do I have to reinstall?

Never reinstall. Download the new zip, unzip it over your existing extension folder, and reload the extension — your settings survive because they live in your browser, not the folder. The extension tells you when an update is out with a gold NEW badge. Full instructions here →

Will it alert me for my own replies?

No. Outgoing messages are detected and filtered out, so you're only pinged when a buyer writes to you.
