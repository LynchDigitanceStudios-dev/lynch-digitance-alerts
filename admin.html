Maintenance mode off

While it is on, every extension shows UNDERGOING MAINTENANCE (popup banner, tag on each alert, and a one-time Telegram notice), and so does the website. Turning it off tells everyone it is back. Reaches installs on the latest build, within about 15 minutes.

Send an update

To everyone's Telegram. Every extension picks this up within about 15 minutes (the browser must be open) and sends it to its own Telegram bot, once. It reaches people without an account too, but only on version 1.15.0 or newer.
